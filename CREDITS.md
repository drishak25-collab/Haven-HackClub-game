## Original content

Industrial skyline SVGs, lantern/checkpoint art, ghost-platform art, signage, interface layout, game text and synthesized sound effects were created for this project with Codex assistance. They are not sourced from Kenney. The platformer concept and direction were supplied by the user. Some unused SVGs from the earlier forest theme remain in art/ for editing.

## Engine

Godot Engine 4.5.1 — MIT license: https://godotengine.org/license/
The playable app bundles the official universal Godot executable. Its complete third-party notices are also available in the Godot editor under About → Third-party Licenses. The project itself does not depend on additional plugins or paid assets.


## The Foreman update

The Foreman character SVG, attack patterns, dialogue, arena layout and audio/foreman-shift.wav percussion/bass loop are original content created for Trashlight with Codex assistance. No Undertale sprites, dialogue, music, or sound effects are included. The arena's platform surfaces and player sprite continue to use the Kenney packs credited above.

The v1.3 laser glow/trails, blast-ring effects, bomber behavior and reactor-blast.wav audio are original procedural content for this project. No additional external assets were used.


## GUI redesign (v1.4)

Visual reference: Tales of Orbis by Jake — https://jakeojeff.itch.io/tales-of-orbis . Its title screen, pixel typography, warm accents and compact corner HUD informed the redesign. Its artwork, logo, music, icons and source code are not included. The industrial night medallion and Trashlight title arrangement are original.

Silkscreen font by Jason Kottke and contributors, downloaded from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/silkscreen . Licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see credits/Silkscreen-OFL.txt. The font is used without modification.


The v1.5 PNG sprite sequences in art/fx/ are original pixel graphics authored procedurally with Godot for this project. They are not Minecraft assets. AnimatedSprite2D performs frame playback at runtime; the existing external-art and font credits remain unchanged.

The v1.6 orbital_charge and orbital_strike PNG frame sequences and redesigned targeting markers are original procedural pixel art for Trashlight. No new third-party assets were added.

The v1.7 steel-spike PNG frames, impact arrangement and volley patterns are original content for this project. Existing Kenney and font credits are unchanged.
